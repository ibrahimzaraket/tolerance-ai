Executive Summary

ToleranceAI — Turning 400h / year of commute dead time into productive micro-growth

A mobile app that converts the ~400 hours per year a European commuter spends in transit into structured, measurable personal growth — built natively for the constraints of public transport (one-handed use, offline-by-default, variable ride duration, noise tolerance).

Concept & Products

Where Spotify, Duolingo and Blinkist were designed for laptops and squeezed into a phone, ToleranceAI is built natively for the commute. Three product pillars:

① Adaptive Duration Engine

GPS- and behaviour-driven algorithm that auto-sizes content to ride length and user energy.

② Offline-First Library

Pre-fetched packs that survive tunnel dead zones (e.g. Paris Métro line 4).

③ Habit-Loop Gamification

Streaks, XP, certificates and departure-time push — 2× retention vs. category.

Not a podcast, not Duolingo, not a generic LMS. The only product designed around the constraints of public-transport commuting. Offline-first + adaptive ride-fit + the behavioural dataset compound into a moat.

Empathy Map — Target Customer Deep Dive

Beyond the commute context, our research captures the lifestyle of the modern professional that drives our brand voice and content categories. The same audience that demands transparency, time-efficiency and measurable progress in food and wellness expects exactly those qualities from a learning product. Co-founders' joint synthesis below.

What do they
Do?
  • Maintain a busy work & personal life
  • Plan meals and snacks ahead
  • Exercise and prioritise wellbeing
  • Research and try new healthy options
  • Purchase online and in-store
  • Share recommendations with peers
Who
Are we empathising with?

Health-conscious professionals (25–45) seeking plant-based, productive and balanced lifestyles — the same active, growth-oriented audience that powers our commute-learning use-case.

What do they
Say?
  • "I want food / content that fuels me, not slows me down."
  • "I care about what's in my food and where it comes from."
  • "It's hard to find options that taste / feel great."
  • "I value brands that are transparent and sustainable."
What do they
Hear?
  • Friends and family encouraging healthier choices
  • Expert advice on nutrition and environment
  • Conversations about sustainability and ethical brands
  • Influencers and communities promoting plant-based living
What do they
Need?
  • Convenient, nutritious and great-tasting solutions
  • Clean ingredients and proven benefits
  • Trustworthy brands aligned with their values
  • Solutions that fit their busy lifestyle and budget
  • Feel better, have more energy, see real results
What do they
See?
  • A growing variety of plant-based products and brands
  • Information about health, fitness and sustainability
  • Others choosing healthier lifestyles
  • Social-media content and reviews about nutrition and wellness
Pain
Fears, frustrations & anxieties
  • Lack of time to prepare healthy meals / pursue growth
  • Limited availability of tasty, convenient options
  • Uncertainty about ingredient / information quality
  • Higher perceived cost of premium offerings
  • Guilt or frustration when lifestyle goals aren't met
Gain
Wants, needs, hopes & dreams
  • Feel healthier, stronger and more focused
  • Enjoy delicious solutions without compromise
  • Support sustainable and ethical companies
  • Save time with convenient, ready-to-go solutions
  • Inspire others, be part of a like-minded community
Translation to ToleranceAI. Our positioning leans into the language already spoken by this audience — convenience, transparency, measurable results, community — applied to learning during the commute rather than to food at home.
