Section 1

Presentation of the Concept & Products

ToleranceAI is a mobile app that converts the ~400 hours per year a European commuter spends in transit into structured, measurable personal growth — without asking for a single additional minute of the user's day. Where Spotify, Duolingo and Blinkist were designed for laptops or desks and then squeezed into a phone, ToleranceAI is built natively for the constraints of public transport: one-handed use, offline-by-default, variable ride duration, noise tolerance.

Core value proposition. A 7-minute ride becomes a 6-minute sprint. A 40-minute ride becomes a structured module. The app meets your commute — not the other way around.

The product is a single mobile application built on three differentiating pillars:

  • Adaptive Duration Engine — GPS- and behaviour-driven algorithm that auto-sizes content to ride length and user energy.
  • Offline-First Content Library — pre-fetched packs that survive tunnel dead zones (e.g. Paris Métro line 4).
  • Habit-Loop Gamification — streaks, XP, certificates and departure-time push notifications driving 2× retention vs. category average.

Section 3

Business Model & Market Positioning

Hybrid B2B-led freemium model. A free B2C tier acts as the discovery funnel; the premium engine is a B2B seat-licence sold to employers via HR / benefits platforms. The B2C subscription is the safety net, transport operator partnerships are the unique distribution moat.

Customer Segments

B2C primary: Urban employees & students 22–40, commuting 45+ min/day in Paris, London, Lille, Brussels.
B2B primary: Mid-to-large employers (200+) with active L&D budgets.
Excluded by design: car commuters and full-remote workers — the commute trigger is the product.

Positioning vs Competition

Not a podcast, not Duolingo, not a generic LMS. The only product designed around the specific physical and cognitive constraints of public-transport commuting. Offline-first + adaptive ride-fit is the moat: any feature can be copied, but the behavioural dataset compounds.

Section 4

Main Results & Strategic Diagnostic

The market problem was validated quantitatively and qualitatively: hundreds of hours of "wasted transition time" per commuter, default behaviour is passive scrolling, and no existing tool addresses the noise / standing / variable-length / offline constraint set. Twelve raw ideas were generated via brainstorming and stress-tested with the WWHWWW lens, converging on the adaptive-engine + audio-first + employer-channel triad.

The SWOT activity matrix produced four strategic objective families:

  • Offensive (S × O): own the "commute-native learning" category before incumbents notice.
  • Reactive (W × O): license existing content; enter through B2B to bypass weak brand recognition.
  • Defensive (S × T): wrap the adaptive engine in IP; build a privacy-first reputation.
  • Maintenance (W × T): keep burn low, niche tight, freemium tier alive.
Section 5

Development Prospects

An 18-month, three-horizon roadmap, sequenced from the SWOT priority order (Defensive → Reactive → Offensive):

0–6 Months · MVP & Defensive

Ship adaptive engine v1 on Paris & Lille metros. Lock in offline-first IP. Sign two pilot employers (≈ 200 seats). Validate retention curve vs. category benchmark.

6–12 Months · Reactive Growth

License third-party content (TED-Ed-class). Onboard 10 B2B clients (≈ 2,500 seats). Negotiate first transport-operator pilot (RATP or SNCF).

12–18 Months · Offensive Expansion

Expand to London & Brussels. Launch the "Commute-to-Skill" B2B package. Target 50 enterprise contracts and €1M+ ARR.

Always-On · Maintenance

Hedge against remote-work erosion by extending to walking & lunch-break contexts. Maintain freemium funnel. Cap fixed costs < 60% of OpEx.

Our user research extends beyond the commute context into the broader lifestyle of the modern professional. The empathy map below — produced jointly by the four co-founders — captures the values, frustrations and aspirations of the health-conscious, growth-oriented professional: the same demographic that, in transit, becomes our active user. It informs tone, content categories (wellness, sustainability, growth), and brand voice.

Do?

  • Plan meals and snacks ahead

  • Share recommendations with peers

Health-conscious professionals (25–45) seeking plant-based, productive and balanced lifestyles — the same active, growth-oriented audience that powers our commute-learning use-case.
